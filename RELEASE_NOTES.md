# Rexy 360 1.0.4-beta — Alpha Wheels

## New
- **Alpha Wheels in (Alt Inputs).** 1A Alpha Wheels are read straight from their USB port — no mouse emulation. Tick **listen**, then **Bind** pan and tilt and turn the wheels. They work like NODO's wheels: absolute, with **Zero** to make the current position the centre and **range** to set how much wheel turn covers a control. While listening, Rexy 360 puts the wheels in serial mode; their own mode comes back when you stop. Quit AW Translator first — only one app can use the port.
- **Record 10s** (Alpha Wheels) saves ten seconds of everything sent to and from the wheels as a file. If something isn't right, send it to rob@realprogear.com.

## Good to know
- Alpha Wheels support has been tested with simulated wheels, not yet with real ones — please report how it works with yours.
- NODO Inertia Wheels still find their Receiver the same way as before.
